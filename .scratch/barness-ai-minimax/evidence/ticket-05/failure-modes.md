# Ticket 05 failure inventory before changes

Public Client and WithHooks are the confirmed seam (T01). Only HTTP Provider,
Host resolvers/admission and observer are external controlled boundaries.

- A narrowed M3.1 high-only catalog may lose depth from Chat's map-count heuristic.
  Mandatory M3 must remain switch-only; model metadata, not id/count, fixes shape.
- Header/payload/response callback panics can crash the process instead of one
  classified, private terminal with released leases and bounded goroutines.
- MiniMax HTTP/SSE errors may echo prompt/thinking/tool data into diagnostics.
  Unknown 429 must retain bounded retry; undocumented quota codes cannot be guessed
  from native base_resp or free-form words. Real compatible permanent-quota shape
  and diagnostic request-id/trace priority remain explicit live obligations.
- Wrong/disabled bindings, cross-tenant credentials and unauthorized callback
  model/auth/native/tool changes may send requests or read secrets too early.
- Same binding/model/session names across tenants/protocols/accounts/purposes may
  leak cache/native state/headers or mix Results/Observer metadata during retries.
- Callback, caller or returned-map mutation may change frozen request bytes,
  retries, selected credentials/options/catalog or other tenants' calls.
- Cancellation before send/headers/after parsed content, deadlines, malformed
  streams and all resource limits may duplicate terminals or retain resources.
- Mixed load may replace/reduce old workload or raise policy limits; original
  workloads must remain and MiniMax adds concurrent calls under unchanged bounds.
