- **SDK:** `openai-go v3.66.0`'s `ChatCompletionService.NewStreaming` sends the adapter's own body (`WithRequestBody`) with `WithMaxRetries(0)` through the Client's shared `*http.Client`, never `openai.NewClient`. Checked: typed params' `SetExtraFields` (the ExtraFields/ExtraBody mechanism; v3.66.0 has no `WithExtraBody`) cannot keep null/unset/zero and pi's replay fields as plainly as an own DTO; `WithJSONSet` works on the byte body and the SDK itself appends `WithJSONSet("stream", true)`, so stream stays on after a payload callback (pi's does not); the transport is the Client's. The SDK's `ssestream.NewDecoder` splits the SSE events; chunks are read as openai-node reads them for pi, because the typed stream stops at `[DONE]`, fails on `"error": null` and non-object chunks, and lacks pi's reasoning fields and absent tool call indexes (ADR-0013 决策一). pi's default fields (`stream`, `stream_options.include_usage`, `store: false`, `max_completion_tokens`, the developer role) are asserted on the received request by every fixture and compared by the differential.
